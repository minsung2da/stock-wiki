## Compliance stance (D-15, T-04-15)

Because no robots.txt is published, compliance with good-citizen scraping
guidelines is **operator-asserted** through the following controls baked into
`src/collectors/kind/client.py`:

1. **Throttle** — `MIN_REQUEST_INTERVAL_SEC = 1.0` — single-flight monotonic
   throttle ensures ≤1 request/second to `kind.krx.co.kr`.
2. **Identifying User-Agent** — a browser-like UA with `stock-wiki-collector`
   suffix; a minimal custom UA was empirically rejected by WAF during probe.
3. **Daily-batch cadence** — Phase 9 systemd.timer runs `stock collect kind`
   once per day. No polling, no concurrent workers.
4. **Failure semantics** — any parse drift raises `ParseError` and marks
   `heartbeat.kind_parse_error=true` (D-17); the collector does NOT silently
   retry or hammer the endpoint on schema changes.

<objective>
Implement the D-04 stance-change gate (SC#6): the deterministic, no-LLM decision of
whether a ticker needs a full 3-role debate or just a lightweight refresh. This is
the token-economics gate that keeps steady-state HOLD reruns cheap while guaranteeing
event-driven (PEAD) tickers get re-debated when something actually changes.

Purpose: SC#6 + D-04. Full debate triggers on new filing/event, price/flow spike,
card expiry/near-expiry, assumption break/invalidation, or an N-day safety net;
otherwise a lightweight refresh re-validates the prior card without spending the LLM.
Keeping the gate LLM-free and isolated makes the "skip the debate" guarantee testable
by asserting no sub-agent is ever constructed on the refresh path.

Output: `src/analysis/gate.py` + `tests/analysis/test_gate.py`.
</objective>

<objective>
Build the daily briefing generator: collect only what CHANGED across decision_cards, rank by
urgency-of-human-review, render the locked 6-column table, and persist it as a
`report_type='daily_briefing'` row. Deterministic and LLM-free — every field is extracted
directly from card structure (D-06), so the render is pure string assembly.

Purpose: this is the heart of Phase 5 (SC#1/2/3/6 + D-01..D-06). It surfaces "what needs your
attention today" — a stance flip (the AI's read reversed) ranks above a strong but unchanged
card. Locks the payload `entries[]` schema the weekly roll-up consumes.
Output: src/briefing/daily.py with change detection, prioritization, render, and orchestrator.
</objective>
